# Login requirement documentation
This document describes the login requirements for the application. It outlines the necessary steps and conditions for users to successfully log in to their accounts.

## 1. User Credentials
- Users must provide a valid email address and password to log in.
- The email address must be in a proper format
- Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and contain a mix of letters, numbers, and special characters.

## 2. Account Verification
- Users must verify their email address before they can log in. A verification link will be sent to the user's email upon registration.
- Users must click on the verification link to activate their account before they can log in.

## 3. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- Users have the option to enable two-factor authentication for added security.
- If 2FA is enabled, users will be required to enter a verification code sent to their registered email or mobile device in addition to their password during the login process.

## 4. Account Lockout
- After five unsuccessful login attempts, the user's account will be temporarily locked for 15 minutes to prevent unauthorized access.
- Users will receive an email notification if their account is locked due to multiple failed login attempts.

## 5. Password Reset
- Users can reset their password if they forget it by clicking on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
- A password reset link will be sent to the user's registered email address, allowing them to create a new password.

## 6. Session Management
- Users will be automatically logged out after 30 minutes of inactivity for security reasons.
- Users can choose to stay logged in for a longer period by selecting the "Remember Me" option during login, which will keep them logged in for up to 7 days unless they manually log out.

## 7. Security Measures
- All login attempts will be logged for security monitoring purposes.
- Users are encouraged to use strong, unique passwords and to change their passwords regularly to enhance account security.
- The application will implement measures to protect against common security threats such as brute-force attacks and phishing attempts.

## Conclusion
By adhering to these login requirements, users can ensure the security of their accounts and have a smooth login experience. It is important for users to follow these guidelines to protect their personal information and maintain the integrity of their accounts.
